## 2. One-liner

GenLayer validators compare a repair quote with proposed extra work and record whether the work is included, out of scope, or unclear.

## 3. Description

When a repair bill grows, clients and providers can disagree about what the original quote covered. Repair Scope Check compares the agreed quote with the proposed extra work. A GenLayer Intelligent Contract asks independent validators to assess the same text, records the verdict, and checks any excerpt against the quote. Visitors can connect a test wallet, submit a case, track its status, and verify the result in Bradbury Explorer. The result is a documented starting point for a scope discussion.

3. Wait for the recorded result. Open **View transaction in Explorer** to confirm the contract and final status.
